    Good to know

    I've never played. Can I still come?

    Yes. The courts welcome beginners and experienced players, and many open play sessions are for all levels. You can rent a paddle or racket at the venue.

    Can I come alone?

    Yes. Open play is made for this. Register for a session and you'll play with whoever else signed up.

    Can I change or cancel a booking?

    All court bookings are final, so check your date and time before you pay. Open play sessions have no cancellations, refunds or rescheduling.

    How much is paddle or racket rental?

    A pickleball paddle is ₱100 and a badminton racket is ₱50. The ball machine is ₱500 an hour.

    What does Instant Replay mean on the booking page?

    Pickleball Courts 1 to 5 are listed with Instant Replay. Ask the front desk how it works on the day you play.

    Can I hold an event or a tournament here?

    The venue hosts events. Message the Facebook page with your date, group size and how many courts you need.
